iii.

Nothing decorative

If an element isn't carrying meaning, it gets cut. Restraint is the cheapest form of craft.

About

Small on purpose.

Fieldnote started in 2019 with a shared irritation: good companies paying for design that flattered them and explained nothing. Six years on, the studio is still two people and still asks the same first question — what is this actually for?

We work in weeks, not quarters. Strategy is written, identities are drawn in the open, and every deliverable is handed over as something your team can operate. No black boxes, no retainer you can't leave.

Most of our clients come from a previous client. That is the only marketing we've ever been any good at.

Principles

  1. Say the true thing first. Clarity beats cleverness in every medium we touch.
  2. Design for the second year. Systems outlive launch weeks.
  3. Show the work early. Ugly drafts, honestly discussed, save months.
  4. Leave a manual. Handover is part of the job, not an upsell.

The shape of an engagement

Week 1–2

Read and ask

Interviews, audit, a written point of view you can argue with.

Week 3–6

Decide and draw

Two directions, one chosen, refined in the open with your team.

Week 7–10

Build and hand over

System, files, and a session where your team runs it alone.
